# Pronunciation audio — sources & attribution

Verb Dojo combines browser speech synthesis, local pre-recorded synthetic audio, and selected human recordings. Human audio is used only for specific verified grammatical readings.

## Verified human recordings

- **read (base form /riːd/)** — [*En-uk-to read.ogg*](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-uk-to_read.ogg). Recorded by Judith Franck, Shtooka Project / Association Shtooka, London accent. **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States (CC BY 3.0 US)**. Linked and streamed unchanged from Wikimedia Commons. [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).
- **read (past and past participle /red/)** — [*En-us-read-past.ogg*](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-read-past.ogg). Recorded by Dvortygirl, United States accent. **Public domain**. Reused for both V2 and V3 because their spoken forms are identical. Linked and streamed unchanged from Wikimedia Commons.

**Important:** The past recording is American English rather than British English. The phonemic contrast /riːd/ versus /red/ is the same in both. These examples are isolated-word recordings, not contextual sentences.

If a human recording fails to load or play, the application falls back to the existing contextual speech-synthesis playback. Other verbs continue to use locally supplied synthetic recordings where installed, otherwise the browser's voice; no other human recordings have been verified.

To replace an audio source, check the particular verb form, pronunciation, speaker variety, rights, and playback reliability before changing the mapping.

## Additional verified human recordings (V18)

The following recordings are hosted on Wikimedia Commons and are streamed without modification. Each item is attributed to its recorder and licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (among other offered licenses).

- **led** (/led/), used for **lead V2 and V3**: [En-us-led.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-led.ogg), Dvortygirl, US English, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- **bear** (/beə(r)/), used for **bear V1**: [En-us-bear.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-bear.ogg), US English, CC BY-SA 3.0. This homophone is acceptable even if the recording's lexical meaning is the animal.
- **was** (/wɒz/ in accented British English, /wʌz/ in many US pronunciations), used for **be V2**: [En-us-was.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:En-us-was.ogg), Dvortygirl, US English (stressed form), CC BY-SA 3.0.

The tool deliberately does not map unverified URLs for other verb forms. Remaining forms use local pre-recorded audio where available or contextual browser synthesis. Human recordings are **not** all British; the accent and availability are disclosed rather than implied.
